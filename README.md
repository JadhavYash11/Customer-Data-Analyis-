# Customer-Data-Analyis

📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes customer shopping behavior using transactional data of 3,900 purchases. The goal is to identify trends, understand customer segments, and provide business recommendations to improve sales and customer engagement

📊 Business Problem
A retail company wants to understand how factors like discounts, reviews, shipping type, age group, and subscription status affect customer purchases and loyalty

🔍 Key Analysis Performed
Revenue by gender
Subscribers vs non-subscribers comparison
Top-rated and best-selling products
Discount impact on purchases
Customer segmentation (New, Returning, Loyal)
Revenue by age group
📈 Dashboard
An interactive Power BI dashboard was created to visualize customer insights and trends.
💡 Business Recommendations
Improve subscription benefits
Strengthen loyalty programs
Optimize discount strategies
